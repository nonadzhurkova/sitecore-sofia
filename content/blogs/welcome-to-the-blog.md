Until now, the best way to learn what the Sitecore Sofia community has been up to was to catch one of our meetups.
Starting today, we're adding a blog so members can share write-ups, deep dives, and lessons learned any time —
not just on event nights.

## What kind of posts fit here

- Deep dives into a Sitecore feature you've been working with
- Write-ups from a talk you gave (ours or elsewhere)
- Lessons learned from a tricky production issue
- Tooling and workflow tips relevant to the community

## How to publish a post

Posts are plain Markdown files living in the repository at `content/blogs/`. To add one:

1. Copy `content/blogs/_TEMPLATE.md` to `content/blogs/<your-slug>.md`.
2. Fill in the frontmatter (title, date, author, tags, excerpt).
3. Write your post in Markdown.
4. Open a pull request.

No login, no CMS — just a PR, the same way we add new events to the site.
